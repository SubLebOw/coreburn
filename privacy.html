COREBURN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

COREBURN ("the game", "we") is a free action game for Android and the web, made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information is collected when you play and why.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email, location or contacts.
  • Your best score and sound setting are stored only on your own device.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data (like your advertising ID) to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • The website version (sublebow.github.io) shows no ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, best wave and whether sound is muted in your device's local storage. This never leaves your device and we can't see it. Uninstalling the app (or clearing site data in your browser) deletes it.

2. Advertising (Android app only): Google AdMob

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between runs and optional rewarded ads (for example, "watch an ad to revive"). To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android), app instance IDShowing ads, frequency capping, measuring ads, preventing fraud. Personalised ads only where you have allowed it.
IP address (used to estimate rough location, e.g. country)Choosing relevant ads, legal compliance, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device; analytics and diagnostics
Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and crash/performance logs of the ads SDKAd measurement, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads (using Google's consent tool). You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" link on the title screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

3. Game updates (Android app only)

When the app is online it checks sublebow.github.io (hosted by GitHub) for a newer version of the game and downloads it. Like any web request, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive personal data from these checks.

4. The website version

The browser version is hosted on GitHub Pages. It shows no ads and uses no cookies or analytics of its own. GitHub may log your IP address for security purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement above).

5. Children

COREBURN is not directed at children under 13 and contains cartoon fantasy violence. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone except as described above (Google AdMob for ads).

7. Security and retention

We don't run our own servers or store your personal data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
